Who HostCompare Is For

Whether you are launching your first WordPress blog, migrating an established ecommerce store, or architecting a scalable cloud cluster, HostCompare provides clear, side-by-side technical breakdowns. We cover shared hosting, managed WordPress, VPS, and cloud infrastructure across major global providers.

How Comparisons Are Created

Every provider is evaluated against standardized criteria: server software (LiteSpeed, Nginx, Apache), storage hardware (NVMe SSD vs standard SSD), backup frequency, SSL automation, customer support availability, and pricing transparency. Read our complete Comparison Methodology for full details.

How We Are Funded

HostCompare may earn a referral commission when you purchase through links on our site — see our Affiliate Disclosure for details. Affiliate partnerships never influence our ratings or verdicts; providers cannot pay for higher scores. We also display clearly labeled non-intrusive advertisements (see our Cookie Policy).
